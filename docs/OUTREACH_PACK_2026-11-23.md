## 2. The free tools

Two tools, one index: https://codenest.uk/tools/

- **Startup Runway Calculator** — https://codenest.uk/tools/runway-calculator/ — cash, burn and revenue growth in; months of runway and when to start raising out.
- **Technical Due Diligence Readiness Check** — https://codenest.uk/tools/technical-due-diligence-check/ — 25 statements across seven areas investors examine; scored in the browser, nothing sent anywhere.

Both are free with no sign-up. Lead with that: resource pages reject anything that looks
like a lead-capture funnel. (The runway calculator offers to email a projection, but only
if the user asks for it.)
